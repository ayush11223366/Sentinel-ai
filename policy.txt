# RAZORPAY MERCHANT ACCEPTABLE USAGE POLICY (AUP)

1. PROHIBITED BUSINESS CATEGORIES
Razorpay strictly prohibits the use of its payment gateway for the following business types:
- Cryptocurrency trading, exchanges, or initial coin offerings (ICOs).
- Online gambling, casinos, sports betting, or lotteries.
- Adult entertainment, pornography, or escort services.
- The sale or distribution of pirated, cracked, or unlicensed software, including keygens and unauthorized digital downloads.

2. HIGH-RISK BUSINESS CATEGORIES (Requires Manual Review)
The following categories are permitted but must be flagged as High Risk:
- Drop-shipping e-commerce stores.
- Financial consulting promising guaranteed high returns.
- Pharmaceutical sales and online pharmacies.

3. LOW-RISK CATEGORIES
Standard e-commerce, local service providers, licensed software distributors, and educational platforms are generally considered low risk.
